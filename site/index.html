Common questions

Does MedCurated replace our EHR?

MedCurated adds documentation tools to your existing hospital information system (HIS) or electronic health record (EHR). Integration options depend on your system.

Who reviews and signs the documentation?

The clinician reviews, corrects and signs each draft.

Which languages and specialties are supported?

Confirm your required languages and specialty templates with the team. Request examples and evaluation results for your use case.

Can we deploy on our own infrastructure?

Yes. Discuss hospital hardware or private cloud deployment, including capacity, network requirements and support access.

What should we bring to the demo?

Your current HIS or EHR name, the workflow you want to improve and the specialties and languages you need. No patient records are needed for the initial discussion.

How is pricing determined?

Request a quote for your workflows and deployment. Ask for hardware, integration and support costs to be itemized.
